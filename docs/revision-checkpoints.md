# Revision checkpoints

The brief includes two rounds of reasonable revisions. This document records the review structure only. It does not represent a quote, a delivery date or client acceptance.

## Round 1: layout and design
- Overall direction: typography, palette, photography mood and section layouts.
- Page structure and navigation (including the "Therapy" grouping).
- Mobile and tablet presentation.
- Output: one consolidated list of changes, agreed before work continues.

## Round 2: refinements and final checks
- Copy placement once the client's supplied content is in.
- Image crops with the client's photography.
- Booking and contact integration, form behaviour, SEO titles and descriptions.
- Final QA: links, accessibility, performance, redirects.
