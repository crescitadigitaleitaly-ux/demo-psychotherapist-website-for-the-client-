## Accuracy decisions
- EFT is always expanded as **Emotionally Focused Therapy**. The EFT page and FAQ explicitly separate it from Emotional Freedom Techniques (tapping).
- No efficacy statistics are quoted. Secondary websites cite improvement percentages, but these were not verified against primary research, so the site only says EFT is "described by its training organisations as evidence-based".
- Session counts ("8–20 sessions" in secondary sources) are deliberately not stated. The copy says length varies.
- Couples copy never promises that a relationship will be preserved, and it states that therapy may help some couples separate with more understanding.
- Confidentiality is described with its limits, never as absolute.
