Demo privacy information

This page describes how this concept demonstration website actually behaves. It is not a privacy policy for a real practice.

About this website

“The Relational Practice — Concept” is a fictional identity used to demonstrate the design of a psychotherapy practice website. It does not represent a real therapist or service, and it does not offer appointments.

What this demo does with information

  • No enquiries are collected. The enquiry form on the Contact page is a disabled preview with fictional sample values. It cannot be filled in or submitted, and the “Preview enquiry confirmation” button only displays a message on your screen.
  • No cookies, analytics or tracking. The site does not set cookies, use analytics, advertising pixels or third-party scripts, or store anything in your browser.
  • No third-party embeds. Fonts and images are served from the same location as the pages. There are no maps, booking widgets or social media embeds.
  • Hosting. Like any website, the server that hosts this demo may keep standard technical access logs (such as IP address and pages requested). Hosting provider and log retention to be confirmed at deployment

Search engines

Every page asks search engines not to index it. This reduces the chance of the demo appearing in search results, but it is not a form of access protection: anyone with the link can view the pages.

Production privacy policy — pending

A production website that collects enquiries would need its own privacy policy, prepared or approved by the practice in line with its obligations under Australian privacy law and its professional requirements. That content has not been written and should not be inferred from this page.

Questions about this demonstration can be directed to the designer, Crescita Digitale Italia.
